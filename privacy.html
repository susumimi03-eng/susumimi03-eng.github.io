Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30

1. What data the App handles

The App stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens and the basic profile identifiers (open id, display name) of the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts, so that it can publish videos to those accounts. No data about any other person is collected.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not transmitted to any server other than the platform APIs (TikTok, Meta) that issued them. The App has no backend server and no database.

3. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok or Instagram account settings, and stored tokens are deleted on request to the contact address below.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and collects no data from them.
